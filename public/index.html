The Real Barrier

Language shouldn't decide who gets to participate.

The world is multilingual. Classrooms, conferences, churches, and public events are filled with people who speak different languages. But most live talks are delivered in just one language.

Current Situation

Today, people who don't speak the primary language often have to:

  • ✕ Wait for slow translations.
  • ✕ Pay for expensive human interpreters and heavy equipment.
  • ✕ Sit in separate rooms or watch different livestreams.
  • ✕ Miss important parts of the talk—or feel completely left out.

"BridgeeAI changes this. One speaker talks naturally, and everyone understands in their own language."

Clarity & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Do listeners need to download an app or create an account? +
No. Listeners just scan a QR code or tap a link to open the page in their phone browser. No app install or sign-up needed.
How do listeners hear translated voice without disturbing others? +
Listeners plug their own headphones or earbuds into their mobile phones.
What if a language doesn't have translated voice audio? +
BridgeeAI automatically switches that listener to fast text captions (Read Mode) and shows a friendly message explaining why.
Can I show captions on a projector or live stream? +
Yes. BridgeeAI includes special links (/venue for projectors and /overlay for software like OBS and vMix).
