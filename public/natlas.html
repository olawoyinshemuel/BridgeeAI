The Real Barrier

Language should not be a barrier to participation.

Multilingual training, conferences, education, and public events can require interpreters or create delays when participants and speakers do not share the same language. BridgeeAI is designed to reduce that communication friction through real-time AI speech processing and live translated captions.

Current Situation

In multilingual gatherings, participants and speakers face:

  • ✕ Consecutive interpretation delays that halve speaker pace.
  • ✕ Regional language exclusion when training is delivered only in foreign languages.
  • ✕ Prohibitive costs for human interpreter booths and heavy audio headsets.
  • ✕ Heavy video streaming tools that buffer and disconnect on slow mobile data.
The BridgeeAI × N-ATLAS Solution
"Speak naturally in your Nigerian language. Every participant understands in their own language in real time."

By combining N-ATLAS speech recognition with BridgeeAI's lightweight real-time delivery layer, speakers present comfortably in their native language while attendees read synchronized captions on their personal phone browser or auditorium screens.

Simplicity in Motion

How BridgeeAI × N-ATLAS Works

A simple 4-step pipeline from spoken Nigerian words to live translated captions.

STEP 01

The Speaker Speaks

The presenter talks naturally into any standard microphone in Yorùbá, Hausa, Igbo, or Nigerian English without pausing or slowing down.

  • ✓ Built-in browser microphone capture
  • ✓ 16kHz mono audio streaming
  • ✓ Hands-free continuous speech
STEP 02

Understand (N-ATLAS)

N-ATLAS processes 250ms streaming PCM chunks and produces phonetically accurate transcript tokens with full tone marks and diacritics.

  • ✓ Specialized Nigerian acoustic models
  • ✓ Diacritics and tonal accuracy
  • ✓ Sub-second token generation
STEP 03

Translate (BridgeeAI)

BridgeeAI routes finalized transcripts through its low-latency translation layer with session-specific domain glossaries.

  • ✓ Multilingual translation engine
  • ✓ Technical term and acronym preservation
  • ✓ Synchronized sentence delivery
STEP 04

Deliver & Follow Along

Participants follow along on personal phones, auditorium stage screens, or broadcast overlays with data usage under 2 KB/s.

  • ✓ Instant QR code joining (no app needed)
  • ✓ Live venue projector display mode
  • ✓ Mid-session language switching

End-to-End Pipeline

System Architecture

Clean modular separation ensuring resilient transmission from microphone capture to participant screen.

🎙️

01. Speaker Audio

Host microphone captures 16kHz mono audio and streams 250ms PCM chunks over WebSockets.

🇳🇬

02. N-ATLAS ASR

Processes Nigerian language voice with tonal diacritics into accurate transcript tokens.

🌐

03. BridgeeAI Translation

Orchestrates low-latency multilingual translation with domain-specific terminology preservation.

📱

04. Audience Delivery

Lightweight caption streams (< 2 KB/s) broadcast to participant phones and venue LED walls.

African Infrastructure

Engineered for real-world networks—from 2G to 5G.

BridgeeAI is built to keep working smoothly on actual mobile devices and cellular connections across Africa:

📲

Mobile-First, No Install

Participants join instantly by scanning a QR code or opening a short link. No app download or account creation required.

📶

< 2 KB/s Bandwidth

Captions stream as ultra-lean text packets (~180 bytes) optimized to prevent buffering on congested 2G/3G mobile networks.

If a phone drops network during a cellular handover, it automatically reconnects and resumes captions without refreshing.
