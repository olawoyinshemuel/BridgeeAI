How It Works in 3 Simple Steps

From instructor podium to student devices with zero onboarding overhead.

STEP 01

The Instructor Speaks Naturally

The lecturer speaks into any standard microphone (laptop, podium, or wireless headset). No artificial pauses or changed delivery cadence required.

  • ✓ Natural, uninterrupted teaching delivery
  • ✓ Plugs into existing classroom AV or Zoom audio
  • ✓ No special instructor training needed
STEP 02

BridgeeAI Applies Course Context

Our neural engine transcribes the lecture, matching technical vocabulary against pre-uploaded lecture slides, glossaries, and reading lists.

  • ✓ Technical formulas & academic terms recognized
  • ✓ Real-time grammar & context correction
  • ✓ Parallel multi-language translation pipeline
STEP 03

Students Follow Live & Review After Class

Students scan a QR code on the lecture slide or whiteboard, choose their preferred language, and select:

  • Read: Follow high-contrast translated captions on their mobile screen or laptop.
  • Listen: Hear synthesized voice translation through personal earbuds.
  • Read + Listen: View captions while listening to matching voice audio concurrently.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Do students need to download an app or create an account? +
No. Students simply scan a QR code or click a session link to join instantly in their web browser.
Can students download translated notes after class? +
Yes. Instructors and students can export full transcript logs in their target language at the end of the session.
How does BridgeeAI handle crowded campus Wi-Fi? +
BridgeeAI automatically switches to an ultra-light text fallback mode (<2 KB/s) if network connectivity degrades, keeping captions flowing without crashing.
Can we integrate BridgeeAI with our remote stream software (Zoom, OBS, vMix)? +
Yes. BridgeeAI provides transparent browser overlay links (/overlay) that layer cleanly over remote video streams.
