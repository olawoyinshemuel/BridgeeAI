Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I use BridgeeAI as a transparent overlay on YouTube Live or vMix? +
Yes. BridgeeAI provides a transparent browser source URL (/overlay) designed specifically for OBS Studio, vMix, Wirecast, and broadcast switchers.
Do physical event attendees need to install an app? +
No. Attendees simply scan a QR code displayed on venue signage or stage screens to open live captions directly in their mobile browser.
How does BridgeeAI handle noisy exhibition halls? +
Feeding audio directly from the speaker's microphone or mixing console ensures clear audio input, bypassing ambient crowd noise.
Can we customize the visual style of the captions on our stream? +
Yes. You can customize font sizes, colors, background box opacity, line wrapping, and screen positioning directly from the overlay setup console.
