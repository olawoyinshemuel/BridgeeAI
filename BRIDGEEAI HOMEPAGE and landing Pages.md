# **SECTION 2 — THE PROBLEM**

## **Language shouldn't decide who gets to participate.**

The world is multilingual.

Classrooms are multilingual.  
Conferences are multilingual.  
Churches are multilingual.  
Events are multilingual.

But live communication is still often designed around one language.

That means people may need to:

* wait for interpretation;  
* rely on human interpreters;  
* use separate equipment;  
* miss parts of a conversation;  
* choose between languages;

One speaker can communicate naturally while every participant chooses how they want to understand.

# **SECTION 5 — BUILT FOR THE REAL INTERNET**

## **Not everyone has perfect connectivity. BridgeeAI doesn't assume they do.**

BridgeeAI is designed for real-world network conditions—from **2G to 5G**.

When connectivity is strong, participants can use text and translated audio.

When connectivity becomes limited, BridgeeAI prioritizes lightweight translated text.

If a connection drops, BridgeeAI attempts to reconnect while preserving the participant's language and experience.

### **Designed for:**

**2G · 3G · 4G · 5G · Wi-Fi**

> The goal isn't to make everyone use the same experience.  
> The goal is to keep everyone connected to the message.

# **SECTION 17 — FAQ**

### **Do participants need an account?**

No. The core participant experience is designed to work through a QR code or link without requiring an account.

### **Do participants need to install an app?**

No. The core experience runs in the browser.

### **Can participants choose different languages?**

Yes. Each participant can independently choose their language.

### **Can a participant change language during a session?**

Yes. Participants can switch languages without leaving the session.

### **Can participants listen instead of reading?**

Yes. Where audio translation is supported for that language, participants can choose Listen or Read \+ Listen.

### **Does BridgeeAI support African languages?**

BridgeeAI is designed with strong African language support as a product priority. Available capabilities vary by language.

### **Does BridgeeAI work on slow networks?**

BridgeeAI is designed for variable connectivity from 2G through 5G, with text-first and adaptive experiences for constrained networks.

### **Can I use BridgeeAI with OBS or vMix?**

BridgeeAI can provide browser-based overlays designed for production workflows such as OBS and vMix.
